The grating element

A diffraction grating contains thousands of equally spaced slits. Their spacing d controls the angles at which bright maxima appear.

d = 1 / N15,000 lines/in → 1.693 μm spacing

Orders of diffraction

Bright maxima occur at integer orders. Higher orders spread farther from the central maximum, until no solution is physically possible.
